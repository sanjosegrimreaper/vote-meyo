From the Developer

A message from the developer, about why Mr. Meyerowitz deserves to win.

The kind of teacher Mr. Meyerowitz is has you looking forward to coming to class. Whatever he has planned will be worth your while. There isn't a single time when I've left his classroom feeling as though I had simply endured another period. His passion and dedication to what we're doing is evident, and that energy feeds into us.

His Colonial and Post-Colonial History class has completely altered the way I view history. In his class, we deconstruct and analyze historical perspectives. We debate each other, and relate events from the past to contemporary issues. This type of approach creates a connection to reality rather than isolating historical events within a textbook.

I also enjoy the debates in his class. I have never attended a class where students actually cared about what they were discussing. Students listened to each other, challenged each other's views, and then walked out of the class feeling they had learned something new. This does not occur without a conscious effort.

There is an unexplainable sense of community among classmates that exists in Mr. Meyerowitz' classroom. We are all exploring and learning at the same time. This type of collaborative learning environment is extremely rare, and it was developed early on in his classroom due to the positive culture he established.

Early on this year, Mr. Meyerowitz experienced a very challenging situation personally. While I am not revealing too much detail regarding the specifics of his experience since that would be his decision to share or not; however, throughout the process he continued to provide us with the same level of commitment and enthusiasm. He continued to post humorous Schoology updates (for those familiar with them). He continued to record video lectures to ensure none of us fell behind. He consistently demonstrated that even during difficult times, coming to school felt worthwhile. To say that shows character is an understatement.

Through Mr. Meyerowitz's class, I learned how to engage in critical thought processes, learn how to question assumptions, and see firsthand what it means to genuinely care about what you are doing. As such, I believe I'm a better student today because of him, and I suspect there are many others who would agree with me.

So if you have taken the time to read this message, please consider voting for Mr. Meyerowitz. It's rare that teachers of his caliber exist, and this may be our opportunity to demonstrate that we appreciate his efforts.

— Aryav K.
