how supporters can help

Voting, explained

Everyone who wants to support Mr. Meyerowitz can vote for free once every 24 hours with a Facebook account through bit.ly/vote-for-meyo. It only takes a few seconds, but those daily votes really add up and can make a huge difference over time.

Share the link with friends, family, and classmates, remind people to come back each day, and help spread the word. Let's show how much Mr. Meyerowitz means to our community and help him get the recognition he truly deserves.

A strong campaign is built on consistency. If even a small group of supporters makes voting part of their daily routine, that momentum can grow quickly and carry Mr. Meyerowitz through each round.

a quick bio

Meet Mr. Meyerowitz

Mr. Meyerowitz has been an educator for twenty years in Phoenix, AZ, and in San Jose, CA. In that time, he has taught about twenty unique Humanities courses—APs, electives, post-APs, and Capstones—and enjoys creating classes that match his students' ever-changing interests. He also loves animals (he currently has five pets plus two foster kittens), The Simpsons, and the histories of post-colonial lands and peoples. In his free time (is time ever free?), you'll find him with a book, on the beach, or scheming to make the Humanities Great Again.
