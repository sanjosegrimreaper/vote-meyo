about the show

What is Passing Period?

Passing Period is an informal YouTube series Mr. Meyerowitz produces to model thoughtful, education-forward content for students online, and to introduce his teaching style to students at BISV and beyond who haven't yet had him as their teacher.

"The goal is to help students see positive role models online so that they look for other educated 'experts' and use their social media time to become more educated and engaged individuals," he has explained.

If you've got a question about history, literature, the humanities, current events, or anything else you'd want him to take on, send it through the form above. Everyone is welcome to contribute.
